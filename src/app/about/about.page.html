SUSBRO - Who’s the Spy?

ABOUT OUR GROUP

Project description

A pass-the-phone social deduction game designed for quick group play on a single Android device. Players share clues while one hidden spy tries to blend in and uncover the secret word.

How to Play

Read the game rules
  1. Pass the phone to each player for a private role reveal. Keep your role secret.
  2. Civilians see the secret word. The spy sees the theme and tries to blend in.
  3. Give one clue on your turn. Do not say the secret word. The timer ends each turn.
  4. Call one emergency meeting if needed, then vote privately. You cannot vote for yourself.
  5. If the spy is identified, they get one final guess. A tied second vote means the spy wins.
